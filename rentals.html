Some of our most popular rentals:

  1. Beast Crawler 9x: A powerful mountain bike with 20 gears, carbon fiber wheels and a solid steel frame. A fantastic choice for intermediate to advanced bikers looking for a reliable ride.
  2. Mountain Snake 500T: For the younger adventurer, this mountain bike is perfect for someone aged 9-12 years old. The aluminum alloy wheels make sure the bike doesn't stop for anyone, and it comes with 14 gears to suit your preferences.
  3. Devil's Chariot: Sometimes referred to by our employees as a Harley that doesn't need gas, this is the premier adult-sized street bike. Boasting 24 gears and a custom flame-wrap, this ride is sure to get you nods of approvement from all cyclists, whether or not they require gas.
  4. 801 Pebble Hopper: One of the best starter bikes we have, this pint-sized powerhouse is perfect for any 6-10 year old interested in finally riding alongside the big boys. With 7 gears, a comfortable polyester seat, and grippable handlebars, this bike will make any youngster an absolute convert to the wonders of road biking.

Rental terms:

All bikes have a fixed charge of $25 per day rental, plus an additional $5 for every hour in use after the first two hours, during which time no additional charges are given. In the case of a week rental, the sole expense is $150 per bike.

Bringing your own helmet is recommended, but they can also be rented for $10 a helmet. In the case of damage to either the bike or helmet, additional charges will be applied depending on severity.

Rental bikes vary greatly in size, so don't hesitate to call in advance to make sure it suits you!
